# narai. privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Max Miller. Support: max@miller-max.com.

The Store edition processes imported videos, subtitles, dictionary searches, playback position and saved vocabulary on your device. It has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and sends no media or dictionary searches to a publisher server. Japanese–English and Japanese–German definitions are bundled from Kotoba Atlas, including JMdict and other attributed sources. Pronunciation uses Apple's system text-to-speech.

You choose files through Apple's document picker. A cloud-file provider you choose may download those files; that provider's terms and privacy policy apply. The app copies imported videos into its private storage. Remove a video in the app to delete that copy and its subtitles. Saved vocabulary is removed separately. Deleting the app removes its local data; copies and backups you control may remain under your Apple backup settings.

If you choose Send to Anki, the app passes the selected expression, definition, and example text to AnkiMobile on this device. If you request configuration information, the app reads the Anki-specific clipboard payload returned by that request. The Sync button opens AnkiMobile's own sync action. AnkiMobile handles any syncing under its own settings and policies.

Support, source and license links open your email app or browser. Those providers receive their usual network information. If you email support, the publisher receives the message and uses it to respond. Do not include private media, passwords or unnecessary personal information. Ask max@miller-max.com to delete support messages when resolved.

The separately distributed full community editions have additional streaming, local-server and capture features. This policy applies to narai. (formerly Immersed. on the App Store). See the [community privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for the direct-download Mac edition.
